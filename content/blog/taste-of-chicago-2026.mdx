## When & where
- **Wednesday July 8 - Sunday July 12, 2026**
- **Grant Park**, on the Chicago lakefront (Loop / South Loop)
- **Admission: free** — no tickets; buy food & drink from vendors (cash or card)

## The 2026 music lineup
Free shows at Grant Park headlined by Chicago hip-hop icon **Common**, R&B legend **Babyface**, indie-pop favorites **Beach Bunny**, Grammy-winning reggae artist **Koffee**, and Mexican pop-rock star **Julieta Venegas**.

## Tips
- **Go hungry, bring a card** — most vendors take card, but a little cash helps.
- **Weekday visits are calmer** — Wednesday and Thursday are the least crowded days.
- **Take the L** — Red, Blue, Brown, Orange, Green, and Pink lines all stop within a few blocks; skip driving.
