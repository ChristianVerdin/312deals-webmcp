## The names that built the scene

- **Half Acre** (North Center / Bowmanville), the canonical Chicago craft brewery; Daisy Cutter is a city staple
- **Revolution Brewing** (Logan Square / Avondale), the big independent, with a huge taproom and the Anti-Hero IPA
- **Pipeworks, Maplewood, Begyle, Off Color, Dovetail**, the heart of the neighborhood-brewery middle class, each with its own following
- **Goose Island**, the famous one; the Fulton Street and Salt Shed taprooms are worth a visit even if the purists quibble about ownership

## How to drink it

- **Taprooms**, drink it at the source, often with a food truck parked outside
- **Beer gardens**, summer in Chicago is built for them; many breweries have outdoor space
- **Flights**, the move when you can't decide; most taprooms pour them
