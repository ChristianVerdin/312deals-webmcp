St. Patrick's Day in Chicago isn't a holiday, it's an event. The river goes green, the
bars open at sunrise, and the entire city turns into one massive celebration. This year
it falls on **Saturday, March 14**, which means it's going to be even bigger than usual.

We tracked every St. Patrick's Day food and drink deal we could find across Chicago.
Here's what's out there.

## Key events

**Chicago River Dyeing**, Saturday March 14 at 10 AM. The river gets dyed green
between Columbus Drive and Lake Shore Drive. Best spots to watch: Michigan Avenue
Bridge, Wacker Drive, and any riverwalk restaurant with a patio.

**Downtown Parade**, Saturday March 14 at noon on Columbus Drive. The parade runs
from Balbo Drive to Monroe Street.

## River North highlights

River North is ground zero for St. Patrick's Day. With 33 specials, you won't have
trouble finding a deal. A few standouts:

- **Centennial Crafted Beer & Eatery**, Irish beers, drink and shot specials, and

## Tips for St. Patrick's Day in Chicago

1. **Get there early.** Popular bars start filling up by 9 AM on St. Patrick's Day.
2. **Take the CTA.** Parking downtown is a nightmare. Green Line, Red Line, and Brown
   Line all get you close to the action.
3. **Check the deal times.** Some specials are all-day, others are morning-only or
   evening-only. Confirm with the venue.
4. **Don't sleep on the suburbs.** Naperville, Schaumburg, and Oak Park all have
   St. Pat's deals worth the trip.
5. **Book reservations early.** If you want a sit-down dinner, book now. Brunch
   spots will fill up fast on a Saturday.
