Birria went from a regional Mexican specialty to one of Chicago's defining dishes in just a few years, slow-stewed, chili-rich beef (sometimes goat), folded into a griddled, cheese-crisped tortilla, with a cup of deep-red **consommé** for dipping. When it's done right, it's one of the best few dollars you'll spend in the city.

## How to order birria like you know

- **Quesabirria**, the crispy, cheese-griddled taco; the move for first-timers
- **Consommé**, the dipping broth. Dunk every bite. Some spots sell it by the cup with extra meat.
- **Birria ramen / birria mulitas**, the spinoffs worth trying once you're hooked
- **Goat vs. beef**, traditional birria is goat (chivo); most Chicago spots run beef. Both are legit.

## Where to find it

Birria spread fastest through the **South and Southwest Side** taquerias and a wave of dedicated trucks and pop-ups, Little Village, Pilsen, Back of the Yards, and Brighton Park are strong starting points. Many of the best are small counters and weekend trucks, so hours can be tight; go early before the birria sells out.
