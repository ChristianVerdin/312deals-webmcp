The Cubs opened the 2026 season at home on March 26, a 1:20 PM start against the Washington Nationals. And Wrigleyville did what Wrigleyville does: packed the bars by 11 AM and kept going through the Sweet 16 that night.

Here's how the neighborhood handled the biggest day of the spring.

## The Scene

Opening Day fell on a Thursday, and it wasn't just baseball, the Sweet 16 had games at United Center that same evening. So you had Cubs fans in Wrigleyville at noon, pivoting to college basketball by 6 PM. Some bars ran dual-sport specials all day.

**The game:** Cubs vs. Nationals, 1:20 PM first pitch at Wrigley Field.

The classic Wrigleyville circuit for a day game:

- **Murphy's Bleachers**, The OG. Steps from the bleacher entrance. Open early for every home game.
