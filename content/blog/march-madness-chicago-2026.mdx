The 2026 NCAA tournament brought March Madness to Chicago in a big way. United Center hosted the Midwest Regional, Sweet 16 on March 27 and Elite 8 on March 29, and the city's college alumni bars were packed from the first tip-off to the final buzzer.

Here's how Chicago watched the tournament.

## By the Numbers

- **16 Sweet 16 teams** had alumni bars across Chicago
- **50+ college bars** opened early for noon games
- **100+ game day deals**, bracket challenges, bucket specials, food combos
- **14+ neighborhoods** with college bar presence
- **6 Big Ten teams** in the Sweet 16, the most ever by any conference

## United Center Hosted the Midwest Regional

The biggest draw: two sessions at United Center.

**Sweet 16, Friday, March 27:**
- Alabama vs Michigan at 6:35 PM
- Tennessee vs Iowa State at 9:10 PM

**Elite 8, Sunday, March 29:**
- Winners meet for a trip to the Final Four

## The Thursday Double-Header (March 26)

The wildest day in Chicago sports, Cubs Opening Day at 1:20 PM, then Illinois vs Houston at 9:05 PM on TBS. Wrigleyville went from baseball to basketball without missing a beat. Six Big Ten teams in the Sweet 16 meant every Lakeview bar had a horse in the race.

## Top College Alumni Bars by Team

The classic setup: find your team's bar, claim a seat, and let the specials roll. Lakeview alone had 15+ college bars, making it the undisputed alumni bar capital of Chicago.

**Big draw teams:**
- **Illinois**, Will's Northwoods Inn, Kincade's (Wrigleyville)
- **Michigan**, Duffy's (Lincoln Park)
- **Iowa**, Hawkeye's Bar & Grill (Wrigleyville)
- **Purdue**, Kirkwood Bar & Grill (Lakeview)
- **Ohio State**, Moe's Cantina (River North)
- **Wisconsin**, Will's Northwoods Inn, Four Moon Tavern
