Square Roots is the summer block party that turns Lincoln Square into one big craft-beer-and-live-music street fest. It's back **July 10-12, 2026**, on Lincoln Avenue between Montrose and Wilson, run by the Lincoln Square Ravenswood Chamber of Commerce, and it's one of the best free weekends on the North Side.

## When & where

- **Friday, July 10:** 5 - 10 PM
- **Saturday, July 11:** noon - 10 PM
- **Sunday, July 12:** noon - 9 PM
- **Where:** Lincoln Avenue between Montrose & Wilson, Lincoln Square
- **Cost:** free entry, suggested donation $10 adults / $5 seniors & kids

## The 2026 music lineup

More than 50 acts play across multiple outdoor stages. Headliners include **Margo Price**, Chicago indie favorites **Dehd**, and **Nick Lowe with Los Straitjackets**, plus **Hurray for the Riff Raff**, **JD McPherson**, **Robbie Fulks**, and **Sons of the Silent Age**.

## The food & beer

Square Roots leans hard on local vendors. Inside the gates you'll find **5411 Empanadas**, **Bell Heirs BBQ**, **Baked Cheese Haus**, **Union Dumpling House**, **LC Pho**, **Hello Jasmin**, and **Bad Johnny's** wood-fired pizza. On the beer side, Chicago brewers like **Half Acre** and **Dovetail** pour craft drafts, with cocktails, THC seltzers, and non-alcoholic options too.

The fest is the anchor, but Lincoln Square is a full eating-and-drinking neighborhood, German beer halls, cozy pubs, coffee, and brunch all within a few blocks of the stages. Beat the festival drink lines with a happy hour before you go, or grab brunch before the Saturday and Sunday noon starts:

## Tips

- **Go early on the weekend days** for shorter beer lines and a spot near the main stage.
- **Bring cash** for the suggested donation and some vendors.
- **Take the Brown Line to Western (Lincoln Square)** and skip the parking headache.
