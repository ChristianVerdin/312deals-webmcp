Chicago's rooftop season runs roughly May through September, and locals don't waste a single warm evening. The catch: rooftop drinks usually carry a view tax. The fix is timing, most of the city's best rooftops run a happy hour, and the early-evening light is the best part anyway.

## What makes a great Chicago rooftop

Three things separate a destination rooftop from a glorified patio:

- **The view**, skyline, river, or lakefront (the trifecta is rare)
- **The vibe**, buzzy without being a velvet-rope ordeal
- **The math**, a happy hour or daily special that makes a second round reasonable

## Where to go, by neighborhood

- **River North**, the densest cluster of hotel and restaurant rooftops, and the easiest neighborhood to bar-hop on foot. Browse [River North happy hours](https://www.312deals.com/happy-hours/river-north).
- **West Loop**, Fulton Market's rooftops pair skyline views with the city's best food scene. See [West Loop deals](https://www.312deals.com/neighborhoods/west-loop).
- **The Loop & Riverwalk**, river-level and high-rise both; the Riverwalk's beer-and-cider gardens are the casual, walk-up move.
- **Wrigleyville & Lakeview**, rooftop patios with a neighborhood feel (and a Cubs game across the street). See [Lakeview happy hours](https://www.312deals.com/happy-hours/lakeview).

## The move: go at golden hour

The single best value play on any rooftop is to arrive for happy hour and stay through sunset. You catch the skyline lighting up, the crowd is still manageable, and the drink pricing is at its lowest. Most rooftop happy hours run roughly 3–6pm on weekdays.
