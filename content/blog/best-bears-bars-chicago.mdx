## What makes a great Bears bar?

Three things: **screens, specials, and crowd.**

A proper game day bar needs enough TVs that you're never craning your neck, drink
specials that don't disappear when the game starts, and a crowd that actually cares
about the outcome. Bonus points for food specials, nothing pairs with a fourth-quarter
comeback like half-price wings.
