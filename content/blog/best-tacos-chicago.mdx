Chicago is one of the best taco cities in the country, and it isn't close. Decades of Mexican immigration built a taco scene that runs from no-frills Little Village taquerias to al pastor trompos, suadero tacos, and the birria wave, and you can eat extraordinarily well for very little money.

## Where to start

- **Little Village (La Villita)**, 26th Street is the taco corridor; this is the heart of it
- **Pilsen**, taquerias, taco trucks, and a newer wave of creative spots
- **Back of the Yards & Gage Park**, South Side strongholds with some of the city's best suadero and lengua
- **Late-night taco trucks**, the after-bar move; al pastor off the trompo is the order

## The orders to know

- **Al pastor**, marinated pork off the vertical spit, with pineapple. The benchmark.
- **Suadero**, griddled brisket-cut beef, rich and a little crispy
- **Birria**, the stewed, consommé-dipped beef that took over the city; get the quesabirria with the broth on the side
- **Lengua, carnitas, chorizo**, the rest of the lineup at any serious taqueria
