Mother's Day fell on Sunday May 10 this year, on a 55°F mostly-sunny day after a 70° patio Saturday, the kind of weekend where the city had two distinct rhythms. Saturday belonged to the rooftops. Sunday belonged to brunch.

Here's how Chicago handled it.

## The Scene

Reservations were heavy by Wednesday. The standouts ran multi-day specials, sashimi bouquets at **Sushi-san - River North** from May 8 through May 10, **STK Chicago** running both a Mother's Day brunch *and* a gift-card flash sale ($50 gets $20 bonus, $200 gets $120), and the **Pizzeria Portofino "Brunch by the River"** with an optional tulip bouquet add-on.

River North was the gravity center. Mila Plaza in the West Loop and Gibsons in the Gold Coast picked up the rest of the high-end demand.

## The Best Spots (by what they actually offered)

### Elevated brunch

- **Mila Plaza** (West Loop), Elevated brunch with a curated spring menu. The kind of room where a Mother's Day reservation gets you treated like you booked a Saturday in October.
- **RPM Italian** (River North), Opened early specifically for Mother's Day, with chef-curated specials alongside the regular menu.
- **Pizzeria Portofino** (River North), Avocado toast, Sicilian french toast, optional spring tulip bouquet add-on. The river view is the move.
- **Gibsons Steak House** (Gold Coast), Brunch buffet at The OAKS in Oak Brook, seatings from 10:30 AM. Old-Chicago energy.

### The creative angles

- **Sushi-san - River North**, Sashimi bouquets and tuna roses, May 8-10. Best non-flower-flower idea of the weekend.
- **Pizzeria Uno** (River North), "Mom & Me Deep Dish Pizza Cooking Class" on Saturday May 9 at noon. Booked solid.
- **Kitchen + Kocktails by Kevin Kelley** (River North), Reservation-only Mother's Day dining special.

### India House, best ethnic option

**India House** (River North) ran both a Mother's Day brunch buffet *and* a separate lunch buffet, curated by Chef Pratap Singh. Canacona jackfruit biryani made it onto multiple "best of Mother's Day" round-ups.

### Gift cards & flash sales

A handful of brands turned Mother's Day into a gift-card moment instead of a dining reservation:

- **STK Chicago**, $50 buys $70, $200 buys $320.
- **Benihana** (Streeterville), Same pattern: $10 bonus at $50, $60 bonus at $200, plus a flash-sale "save $20 on $50, save $80 on $200" weekend variant.

If brunch reservations sold out by Wednesday, this was the fallback play.

## The Patio Saturday Bonus

Saturday May 9 hit 70°F and sunny, the first real patio Saturday of 2026. River North rooftops, Wicker Park sidewalk patios, Logan Square beer gardens. Mother's Day weekend essentially started 24 hours early for anyone who could grab a same-day patio table.

This is the weekend the patio guide gets used. Worth bookmarking for next year.

## What Worked, What Didn't

**Worked:** Multi-day specials (May 8-10 instead of Sunday-only) captured the patio-Saturday + brunch-Sunday rhythm. Sushi-san and STK both ran this play.

**Didn't:** Sunday-only reservations sold out Wednesday for the top rooms. If you didn't book by Thursday, you were eating at home or grabbing the gift-card flash-sale fallback.

## Looking Ahead

The takeaway for next year: book by Wednesday for the high-end rooms. Watch for multi-day specials starting Friday. And if Saturday is sunny, switch the plan to a patio lunch, every River North rooftop will run.
